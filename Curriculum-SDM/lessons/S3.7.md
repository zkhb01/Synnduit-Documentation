# S3.7 — Vendor Representatives

## In one line
Vendor Reps are representatives of **3rd-party suppliers** that SDM creates and maintains itself — because HR doesn't — giving each one a **login account and a matching CSSD email, but no other access to CSSD assets**.

## The idea
Vendors are one of the **staffing categories HR doesn't manage** (S1.4). Because there's no system of record feeding them in, SDM **creates and maintains them directly** — this is the same HR-gap pattern that covers temporary jobs and contractors (S3.5), just for a different kind of person.

A Vendor Rep is a person who represents a 3rd-party supplier the district works with. What SDM provisions for them is deliberately narrow:
- a **login account**, and
- a **matching CSSD email** address.

That's the boundary: **no other access to CSSD assets**. A Vendor Rep can sign in and is reachable at a CSSD address, but the account isn't a doorway into systems, files, or sites beyond that.

A concrete example: a supplier sends a technician who needs to be reachable on a CSSD email and able to sign in for a specific engagement. HR never onboards this person, so on the Vendor Reps page you create the rep, and SDM stands up the login and the CSSD mailbox — and nothing more.

## Why it matters
If you don't know SDM owns Vendor Reps, you'll go looking for them in an HR feed that will never contain them — and the rep never gets a login or mailbox. Just as important, knowing the access boundary keeps provisioning correct: a Vendor Rep is **not** a staff member, so granting them anything beyond the login and CSSD email would over-provision an external supplier.

## Watch out for
**"A Vendor Rep is just another contractor."** Both are **HR-gap** categories SDM owns (S1.4), but they're distinct. A **contractor** (S3.5) — a category that also covers student teachers and volunteers — is handled on the Staff side as HR-gap staffing. A **Vendor Rep** is the representative of a **3rd-party supplier**, provisioned only with a login account and a matching CSSD email and **no other access**. Don't treat the two as interchangeable, and don't assume a Vendor Rep gets the access a staff member or contractor would.

## You've got this when you can…
- State that Vendor Reps are representatives of **3rd-party suppliers** and that **SDM** creates and maintains them because **HR doesn't**.
- Name exactly what a Vendor Rep receives: a **login account** and a **matching CSSD email** — and **no other access to CSSD assets**.
- Distinguish a Vendor Rep from a **contractor** (S3.5): both are HR-gap categories SDM owns, but a contractor is HR-gap *staffing* while a Vendor Rep represents a 3rd-party supplier with a narrowly scoped account.
