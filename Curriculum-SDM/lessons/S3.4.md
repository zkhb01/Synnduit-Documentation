# S3.4 — Requesting a Subsite

## In one line
You can't create a SharePoint subsite directly from SDM — you **request** one, and the SPO sync creates it the next time it runs.

## The idea
Creating a subsite under the main Team Site needs **elevated privileges** in SharePoint Online (SPO). SDM doesn't have them: the highest permission delegated to it is **"Contribute"**, and Contribute can't create a subsite.

So instead of creating the subsite live, this page lets a Team Site owner **request** one. The request is queued, and the subsite is actually created the **next time the SPO sync runs** — the same `Sync-*` fleet that propagates data between SDM/DDH and SharePoint. It's a request-and-wait flow, not an instant action.

A concrete example: a CSSD Team Site owner needs a new subsite for a committee. They submit the request here; nothing appears immediately. When the SPO sync next runs, it has the elevated rights SDM lacks, so it provisions the subsite then. The owner then maintains that subsite and its supplemental data.

## Why it matters
If you expect a "Create subsite" button to make the site appear on the spot, you'll think the page is broken when nothing happens right away. Knowing it's a **request** that the sync fulfills tells you the right expectation: submit, then wait for the next sync. It also explains *why* — SDM tops out at Contribute, which by design cannot create subsites.

## Watch out for
**"I clicked it, so the subsite exists now."** No — the page records a *request*. The subsite doesn't exist until the SPO sync runs and creates it with the elevated permissions SDM itself doesn't hold. If the subsite isn't there yet, the sync simply hasn't run; that's expected, not a failure.

## You've got this when you can…
- Explain that SDM can only **request** a subsite because its highest SPO permission is **"Contribute"**, which can't create one.
- State that the requested subsite is created the **next time the SPO sync runs**, not immediately.
- Place this alongside the other domain pages (e.g. Managing Schools, S3.1) as another owner-maintained area, and recall that the `Sync-*` fleet (S1.4) is what bridges SDM and SharePoint.
