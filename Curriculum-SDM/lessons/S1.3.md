# S1.3 — Authorization via AAD groups

## In one line
SDM decides what you can see and do from your **Entra (Azure AD) group membership** — the `DA-*` groups that ride along on your sign-in token.

## The idea
When you open SDM you sign in through **Entra ID using OpenID Connect (OIDC)**. The token Entra returns carries your **group claims** — specifically the **`DA-*` security groups** (e.g. `DA-FNC-CCSD-ADMSDM` for admins, `DA-SCH-…` for school-level roles). Those groups are themselves maintained upstream by `Sync-DDH-AAD`, so SDM doesn't manage them — it just *reads* them off your token.

Authorization then works at two layers:
1. **The menu.** SDM builds your navigation from your group claims. Internally each link maps to a **"management task,"** and a task only appears if one of your `DA-*` groups grants it. Two people can open SDM and see **different menus**.
2. **The API.** Every `/api/*` call re-checks your groups. If you lack the required `DA-*` group, the backend returns **403 Forbidden**.

So a `403` in SDM almost always means one thing: **you're missing the AAD group** for that area — not that the feature is broken.

There's also session handling around this: after sign-in the backend keeps a session cookie (idle timeout, default 1200s) and does **silent token refresh** so you aren't re-prompted constantly. For local dev, `appsettings.json` has **impersonation** settings (a username + AAD groups) so a developer can test *as* a given role without that person signing in — dev-only.

## Why it matters
Almost every "why can't I see X?" or "why am I getting a 403?" question traces back to group membership. Understanding that authorization is **claims-from-AAD-groups**, not something configured inside SDM, tells you where to look (the user's `DA-*` groups) instead of hunting in the app.

## Watch out for
**"Permissions are configured in SDM."** They're not — SDM only *reads* the `DA-*` group claims Entra puts on the token. To change what someone can do, you change their group membership upstream, not a setting in SDM.
