# S3.9 — Temporary Job Assignment

## In one line
The Temporary Job Assignment page is where SDM **owns and creates** the short **temporary jobs (under six weeks) HR doesn't manage**, and where you **maintain Contractor job changes**.

## The idea
Some staffing falls in the **HR-gap** — the categories the HR system of record doesn't manage, which SDM exists to fill (S1.4, S2.4). This page handles two of them:

- **Temporary jobs under six weeks.** These are part of the HR-gap staffing SDM **owns and creates directly** — there's no upstream feed to wait for, so SDM stands the job up itself.
- **Contractor job changes.** Contractors are fully maintained in SDM (S3.5); when a contractor's job changes, you maintain that change here.

Because these are jobs SDM owns rather than augments, this is a **create-and-maintain** surface, not an augment-an-imported-record surface like Schools (S3.1). Like every SDM edit, the result lands in **DDH** with the usual **CreatedBy/UpdatedBy** audit trail, and the temp-job workflow can **fire email notifications** so the right people learn a temporary job happened (S2.4).

A concrete CSSD example: a district-office clerk needs to backfill a three-week records-cleanup assignment that HR will never create a permanent position for. On the Temporary Job Assignment page, SDM creates that short temporary job and assigns the staff member — and when the contractor already covering an overlapping task moves to a different role, you update that contractor's job here too.

This is the **district-wide** counterpart to the school-scoped **School Job Assignment** page (S3.8): S3.8 lets a principal (or delegate) assign **their own school's** staff to temporary jobs from a canned list; S3.9 is for the temporary and contractor jobs SDM owns across the district.

## Why it matters
If you reach for this page expecting it to be school-scoped, or expecting HR to have created the job for you, you'll look in the wrong place. The point of S3.9 is that **these jobs have no system of record** — SDM creates them — and that **contractor job changes live here**, not in an HR feed. Knowing that tells you when to come here (short HR-gap temp jobs, contractor job moves) versus when a principal should use the school self-serve page (S3.8) or when the change really belongs upstream in HR.

## Watch out for
**"This is the same as School Job Assignment."** No — S3.8 is **school-scoped and principal-self-serve** (their school's staff, canned job list). S3.9 covers the **HR-gap temporary jobs SDM owns (under six weeks)** and **Contractor job changes** across the district. Also don't expect to *augment* an HR-supplied job here — these are jobs SDM **creates and owns**, because HR doesn't manage them.
