# S3.2 — Data Owners and delegates

## In one line
The owner of an area — a **manager**, **principal**, or **functional-site owner** — can assign a **delegate** to maintain that area's supplemental data and manage its **SharePoint Online (SPO)** site, because responsibility sits with the owner, not a central team.

## The idea
SDM has three sibling Data-Owner pages (S1.4), one per scope:
- **Department Data Owners** — a **manager** owns the department.
- **School Data Owners** — a **principal** owns the school.
- **Functional Site Data Owners** — an **owner** owns the functional site.

They work the same way and differ only in **scope** (Department / School / Functional Site) and the **owning role** (manager / principal / owner). On each page, the owner can **assign a delegate** to two responsibilities for that area:
- **maintain the area's supplemental data**, and
- **manage the area's SPO (SharePoint Online) site** in the same capacity the owner would.

This is SDM's **RACI self-serve model**: editorial responsibility sits with the people who **own the data** — or with the people the owner **makes delegates** — **not a central team**. Instead of filing a ticket so someone else updates a department's data, the manager who owns it (or their chosen delegate) does it directly, and can hand that responsibility on.

A concrete example: a CSSD **principal** is the School Data Owner for their school. Rather than maintaining everything personally, the principal opens School Data Owners and assigns an office administrator as a **delegate**. That delegate can now keep the school's supplemental data current (the augment-and-link work of S3.1) and manage the school's SPO site **as a principal** would.

Delegate assignments are edits like any other, so they write to **DDH** with the usual **`CreatedBy`/`UpdatedBy`** audit and a **soft-delete** flag (S1.4) — so who delegated whom is attributable and reversible.

## Why it matters
If you assume a central admin team grants every area's access, you'll route requests to the wrong place and wait on a queue that doesn't own the decision. The RACI self-serve model puts that authority with the **owner**: the manager, principal, or owner assigns the delegate. Knowing this tells you exactly who to ask — and that the owner can solve it themselves rather than escalating.

## Watch out for
- **"A central team assigns these delegates."** No — that's the point of the self-serve model. The **owner** (manager / principal / owner) makes the assignment for their own area.
- **Confusing a delegate with an AAD group.** Authorization still runs on the two layers of S1.3 — menus from **Entra `DA-*` group claims** and screen data filtered by the **Permission tables in DDH's `Security` schema** (the RACI-applied access); a delegate assignment is the owner naming who carries the area's data-maintenance and SPO responsibility. Don't conflate the self-serve delegation with the underlying authorization.
- **Treating the three pages as different features.** Department, School, and Functional Site Data Owners are the **same mechanism** at three scopes — only the role name changes.
