# S3.6 — School Staff on external sites

## In one line
School Staff is where you shape the **HR-sourced staff information that appears on external school websites** — giving it school-friendly job labels and the right privacy values.

## The idea
Staff records come from **HR** (see S1.4) — SDM doesn't invent them. The **School Staff** page governs a specific slice of that data: **what shows on the external, public-facing school sites**.

Two things you control here:
- **School-friendly job labels** — HR's job title isn't always what a parent should see. The augment layer lets you apply a friendlier label (e.g. HR's payroll classification becomes "Grade 4 Teacher" on the public site).
- **Privacy values** — what's visible externally versus held back, per staff member.

Because the underlying staff data is HR-sourced and SDM only augments it, every edit writes to **DDH with the usual audit trail** (S1.4) — the change is attributable, and the source data itself stays owned by HR.

There are also **bulk features for resetting values at the start of each new school year**, so you can clear or re-baseline the labels and privacy values across the roster in one pass instead of staff-by-staff.

A concrete CSSD example: at the start of September a teacher who moved schools over the summer is now HR-sourced to her new building. On School Staff you'd use the **start-of-year bulk reset** to clear last year's stale labels and privacy settings, then apply her new school-friendly job label so the external school site shows the right title.

## Why it matters
This is the page that decides what the **public** sees about CSSD staff. Get the privacy values wrong and you over-expose someone's details on a website; skip the start-of-year reset and last year's labels linger on the new roster. Knowing this page is the **external-facing augment** surface — not the place staff records are created — tells you exactly what belongs here.

## Watch out for
**"This is where staff are created or where I fix HR data."** No — staff come from HR, and the broader staff maintenance (FTE augment, contractor full maintenance) lives on the **Staff** page (S3.5). School Staff is narrowly about the **augment data shown on external school sites**: job labels and privacy values, plus the yearly bulk reset. A wrong *source* fact is an HR/sync question, not an edit here.
