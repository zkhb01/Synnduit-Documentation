# S3.8 — School Job Assignment

## In one line
The School Job Assignment page lets a **principal (or their delegate)** assign staff **at their own school** to **temporary jobs** picked from a **canned (predefined) list**.

## The idea
This is a **self-serve** page for the school. The principal — or someone the principal has delegated to act for them (see S3.2 on principals and delegates) — assigns members of **their own school's** staff to **temporary jobs**. The jobs aren't free text: they're chosen from a **canned list** of predefined jobs.

Two things define the page:
- **Who** can use it — the school's principal or their delegate, acting only for that school.
- **What** they can assign — staff at that school, to temporary jobs from the predefined list.

A concrete example: a CSSD elementary principal needs a teacher to cover a short-term lunch-supervision role. On the School Job Assignment page they pick that staff member and select the matching job from the canned list — no IT ticket, no district-office request.

Because the page is **school-scoped**, it only ever touches staff and assignments at the principal's own school. That's the contrast with the **district-wide Temporary Job Assignment page (S3.9)**, which operates across the whole district rather than for a single school.

## Why it matters
Knowing this is the **school-scoped, principal-self-serve** surface tells you who to point at it (the principal or their delegate, not central IT) and what it's limited to (their own school's staff, jobs from the canned list). Confuse it with the district-wide page and you'll send a school-level request to the wrong place — or expect a principal to reach staff outside their school here, which they can't.

## Watch out for
**"This is the district-wide job page."** It isn't. School Job Assignment is **scoped to one school** and driven by that school's **principal or delegate**. District-wide assignment is a **separate** page (S3.9). Also: the jobs are a **canned list** — you select from predefined jobs, you don't invent new ones here.

## You've got this when you can…
- State that **principals or their delegates** assign **staff at their own school** to **temporary jobs** on this page.
- Explain that the jobs come from a **canned (predefined) list**, not free text.
- Distinguish this **school-scoped, self-serve** page from the **district-wide Temporary Job Assignment page (S3.9)**.
