# S1.4 — The core domain entities

## In one line
SDM's menu is a tour of CSSD's organizational data — **people, org structure, and access** — and every edit is written to DDH with an audit trail.

## The idea
The things SDM manages fall into three buckets:

**People & roles**
- **Staff** — teachers, admins, support; with **job assignments**, proxy addresses, and municipal-division links. Includes **temporary / on-hold job** assignments (which can fire email notifications).
- **Vendor Reps** — external contractor/vendor relationships.
- **Trustee Positions** — school-board trustee seats.

**Organizational structure**
- **Schools** — the central unit (name, location, programs, attendance areas, parish).
- **District Programs** — programs offered at one or more schools (e.g. an immersion program).
- **Municipal Divisions** (and their **hierarchy**) and **Wards** — geographic/administrative divisions.

**Access & security**
- **Data Owners** — three AAD-backed security-group families that grant editing rights over an area: **Department**, **School**, and **Functional-Site** Data Owners.
- **Standalone Groups** — static membership lists that *don't* sync from upstream; can be **simple** or **composite** (membership rolls up from other groups).
- **Site Security** — who can access which sites/assets, viewable **by asset** or **by user**.

Across all of these, edits write to the **DDH database** and carry **audit fields — `CreatedBy` / `UpdatedBy`** — so every change records who made it (with an `AuditLog` for history). SDM holds no separate "source of truth" of its own; the authoritative data lives in DDH.

## Why it matters
This is the map of the whole app. When someone says "the school data owner can't be set" or "this standalone group's membership is wrong," you can place it instantly: which bucket, which entity, which menu area — and know the edit lands in DDH with an audit record.

## Watch out for
**Confusing the access entities.** *Data Owners* are AAD-group-backed roles that grant rights; *Standalone Groups* are hand-maintained membership lists (not synced); *Site Security* is the per-site access matrix. They're related but distinct.

## You've got this when you can…
- Sort the main entities into **people**, **org structure**, and **access** buckets.
- Distinguish **Data Owners** (group-backed roles) from **Standalone Groups** (hand-maintained lists) from **Site Security** (per-site access).
- State that edits write to **DDH** with **CreatedBy/UpdatedBy** audit, and that DDH — not SDM — holds the authoritative data.
