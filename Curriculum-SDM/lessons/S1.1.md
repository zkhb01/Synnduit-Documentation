# S1.1 — What SDM is

## In one line
SDM (Supplemental Data Manager) is the **admin portal where humans make the authoritative editorial calls** about CSSD's organizational data — the calls the automated syncs then carry out.

## The idea
CSSD runs a fleet of `Sync-*` tools that mechanically reconcile data between systems — HR, PowerSchool, Entra ID, on-prem Active Directory, and SharePoint — all flowing through the **District Data Hub (DDH)**. Those syncs are great at moving and matching data, but they can't make *judgement* calls: when a new school opens, when a security-group membership needs a correction, when an exception to the rules is required.

That's SDM. It's a web app that lets staff and data-owners **edit the authoritative record in DDH** — add a school, fix a staff job assignment, adjust a group's membership. Those edits then flow back *out* through the sync fleet to AD, Entra, SharePoint, and the rest.

So SDM sits in the middle as the **editorial layer**: `Sync-SRC-DDH` loads bulk data in → **humans correct and extend it in SDM** → the `Sync-DDH-*` fleet propagates the result everywhere.

## Why it matters
If you think SDM "is just another sync tool," you'll misjudge everything it does. SDM is the *one place a person changes the source of truth*. Every feature — schools, staff, data owners, groups — is a controlled way for an authorized human to edit DDH, with the syncs picking up the change afterward.

## Watch out for
**"SDM moves data between systems automatically."** That's the `Sync-*` fleet's job. SDM is the opposite: it's where a **person** makes a deliberate edit. The mechanical movement happens around it, not inside it.
