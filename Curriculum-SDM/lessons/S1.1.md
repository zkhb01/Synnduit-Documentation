# S1.1 — What SDM is

## In one line
SDM manages the **supplemental data** the district needs but DDH doesn't hold — and is the human, self-serve editor where data owners maintain the CSSD-curated parts of DDH.

## The idea
The name is the job: **Supplemental Data Manager**. Its core role — from the start and still today — is to **manage system-of-record sources and augment them with the extra data downstream apps need that DDH itself doesn't hold**. It began as a way to aggregate the small pockets of district data needed to complement those downstream systems, surfaced through data-driven exports from the **District Data Hub (DDH)**.

It's an **evolved** application — its scope widened over time:
- The volatile **template and configuration JSON** files that drive the syncs were moved into SDM.
- It took on staffing categories **HR doesn't manage** — temporary jobs lasting under six weeks, contractors, and vendors.

Throughout, it's designed around a **RACI self-serve model** (a Responsibility Assignment Matrix): editorial responsibility sits with the people who *own* the data — the data-owners team and delegated school staff — rather than a central team keying everything in.

That evolution is why SDM is **now** the day-to-day administrative editor for the CSSD-curated portion of DDH: schools, school staff, district programs, departments, municipal divisions, security groups, standalone groups, trustee positions, vendor reps, wards, site security, and more. The domain it edits is the same domain the `Sync-*` family reads and writes mechanically — but where the syncs reconcile DDH with HR / PowerSchool / Entra / SharePoint automatically, **SDM is where humans make the authoritative editorial calls** that drive what those syncs propagate.

## Why it matters
Two ideas anchor everything else. First, **"supplemental"**: SDM isn't just a window onto DDH — it *holds and manages data DDH doesn't*, plus the config that steers the syncs. Second, **self-serve ownership**: the right person to fix a school's data is the person who owns it, not a central queue. Miss either and you'll misjudge what SDM is for.

## Watch out for
- **"It's just another sync tool."** No — the mechanical movement between HR, Entra, AD, and SharePoint is the `Sync-*` fleet's job. SDM is where a **person** makes a deliberate edit.
- **"It only edits data that's already in DDH."** It does that, but it also manages **supplemental** data DDH doesn't hold — plus the sync template/config JSON, and HR-gap staffing like temporary jobs, contractors, and vendors.
