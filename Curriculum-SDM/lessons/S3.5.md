# S3.5 — Managing Staff (FTE vs Contractor)

## In one line
SDM **augments** permanent FTE staff that arrive from the HR system of record, but **fully maintains** Contractors (paid or volunteer) — creating their security account, email, and an initial job linked to their manager.

## The idea
The Staff page handles two kinds of people, and they're handled very differently.

**FTE (permanent staff) — augment.** Permanent staff come from a **system of record** and are loaded into DDH; the `Sync-*` fleet keeps that imported data current. SDM doesn't create or own these people — it **augments** them, layering on the supplemental attributes the source doesn't provide:
- **Service Area**
- **Support Languages**
- **Proxy Addresses**

This is the same augment-not-own pattern you saw for Schools (S3.1) and in the entity tour (S1.4).

**Contractors (paid or volunteer) — fully maintain.** Contractors don't come from HR — they're part of the **HR-gap staffing** SDM exists to manage (the same group that covers **student teachers and volunteers**, from S1.4). Because no system of record feeds them, SDM maintains them **end to end**: it provisions the **security account and email**, and creates an **initial job linked to the assigned manager**.

On top of those two, the page supports the ongoing churn:
- **User-account changes** for staff.
- **FTE ↔ Contractor conversion** — both directions (a contractor hired on as permanent, or vice-versa).
- **On-Hold name-conflict resolution.** When a **new FTE** comes onboard and their name **conflicts** with an existing record, you resolve it here with the **On-Hold** feature rather than blocking the load. (On-hold/merge events can fire email notifications — see S2.4.)

A concrete CSSD example: a **volunteer reading tutor** starts next week. There's no HR feed for them, so on the Staff page you create the contractor — SDM provisions their account and email and stands up an **initial job tied to their CSSD manager**, and you fill in **Support Languages** so they're routed to the right classrooms. Months later they're hired as permanent FTE: you **convert** the record rather than re-keying it.

Whichever kind they are, the result lands in **DDH** with the usual **CreatedBy/UpdatedBy audit** (S2.3). And critically: **FTEs and Contractors get the same access rights relative to their assigned manager** — the kind of record doesn't change what access they get, the manager does.

## Why it matters
If you treat a contractor like an FTE, you'll wait for an HR feed that never arrives and the person never gets an account. If you treat an FTE like a contractor, you'll try to hand-create someone the system of record already owns. Knowing which lever applies — **augment** vs **fully maintain** — tells you what SDM does for you and what it expects from upstream. And knowing access keys off the **manager**, not the staff type, stops you from over- or under-granting on a conversion.

## Watch out for
**"Contractors are just FTEs without a paycheck."** No — the difference isn't pay (contractors can be paid *or* volunteer); it's **who owns the record**. FTEs are augmented from a system of record; Contractors are **fully maintained in SDM** (account, email, initial manager-linked job). Also don't confuse the **On-Hold name-conflict** step (resolving a duplicate-name *new FTE* at load) with the temporary/contractor **job** workflow (S3.9) — different problems.

- Name the other Staff-page operations: **user-account changes**, **FTE↔Contractor conversion** (both ways), and **On-Hold** resolution of a **new FTE name conflict**.
