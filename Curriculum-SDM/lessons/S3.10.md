# S3.10 — Municipal Divisions and hierarchy

## In one line
Municipal Divisions hold the **city-provided** set of communities, quadrants, and cities that schools service, and the **hierarchy** maintains how communities roll up into quadrants and cities.

## The idea
Two sibling pages work together here:

- **Municipal Divisions** — maintain the municipal division records (the **communities, quadrants, and cities** the city provides) and their supplemental data. This is the set of divisions that schools will be servicing.
- **Municipal Division Hierarchy** — maintain the **parent/child relationships** between those divisions: how **communities roll up into quadrants and cities**. The page also provides a **link to associate the communities a school services**.

So one page keeps the *records*, the other keeps the *roll-up structure* that ties them together — and from the hierarchy you can associate a school with the communities it services.

A concrete CSSD example: the city supplies a set of communities, which roll up into quadrants and then a city. You maintain those records on Municipal Divisions, set the community → quadrant → city roll-up on Municipal Division Hierarchy, and use the link there to associate a given school with the communities it services. (The school itself is augmented on the Schools page — see S3.1 — where Municipal Divisions are linked to the school.)

Like other SDM entities (S1.4), edits write to DDH with the usual `CreatedBy`/`UpdatedBy` audit trail, so each change is attributable.

## Why it matters
If you treat Municipal Divisions and the hierarchy as one undifferentiated thing, you'll look for roll-up relationships on the records page, or try to add a new community on the hierarchy page. Knowing that **Municipal Divisions = the city-provided records** and **Municipal Division Hierarchy = the parent/child roll-up (plus the school-to-communities link)** tells you exactly which page to open for which task.

## Watch out for
**"The records and the hierarchy are the same page."** They're siblings. Municipal Divisions maintains the division records (communities/quadrants/cities) and their supplemental data; Municipal Division Hierarchy maintains the parent/child roll-up and provides the link that associates the communities a school services. Reach for the right one based on whether you're editing a record or its place in the roll-up.

## You've got this when you can…
- State that **Municipal Divisions** maintains the city-provided set of **communities, quadrants, and cities** (and their supplemental data) that schools service.
- State that **Municipal Division Hierarchy** maintains the **parent/child** roll-up — communities rolling up into quadrants and cities — and provides the **link to associate the communities a school services**.
- Pick the right page for a task: editing a division record versus setting its roll-up / associating a school's communities.
