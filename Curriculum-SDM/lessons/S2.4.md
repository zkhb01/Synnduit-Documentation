# S2.4 — Notifications and the temp-job workflow

## In one line
SDM **emails people about staffing changes** it manages — temporary jobs, on-hold merges — using a shared notification library, switched off in dev.

## The idea
Recall from S1.1 that SDM took on staffing categories **HR doesn't manage**: temporary jobs lasting under six weeks, contractors, and vendors. Managing those isn't just data entry — some events need to **notify people**.

SDM sends those emails through the shared **`Cssd.IT.EmailNotifications`** library (MailKit/SMTP). The behaviour is **configuration-driven**:
- `EmailNotificationSettings` holds the SMTP/sender config, and `OnHoldMergeNotificationSettings` lists recipients + job codes for on-hold/temp-job merge notifications.
- On **dev machines**, `DisableEmailNotifications: true` skips actually sending — the email is still written to the audit log so you can see what *would* have gone out.
- In **production/staging**, the servers relay through the corporate SMTP under the app's service-account identity (no SMTP credentials in config).

The temp-job side is a real workflow: create a temporary job, assign the staff member, fill in details, submit (which can fire a notification), and deactivate when it ends. SDM's UI regression suite (`SeleniumTester`) even models it as a family of `TempJob*` steps.

## Why it matters
Two practical takeaways. First, "why didn't the email send?" in dev is **expected** — `DisableEmailNotifications` is on; check the audit log instead of the inbox. Second, the notifications exist *because* SDM owns the HR-gap staffing — they're how the right people find out a temp job or on-hold merge happened.

## Watch out for
**"Email isn't working locally."** On dev it's intentionally disabled; messages are logged, not sent. Don't chase an SMTP bug — flip `DisableEmailNotifications` only where you actually want delivery, and prod/staging use the corporate relay, not config credentials.
