# S3.1 — Managing Schools

## In one line
The Schools page is where you **augment** schools that already exist — adding the supplemental attributes and the links (Attendance Areas, District Programs, Municipal Divisions) the source system never supplies.

## The idea
A school record isn't created in SDM. Schools are loaded into DDH from a **system of record — HRAD** — and the `Sync-*` fleet keeps that imported data current. SDM **never creates or deletes a school**; it edits the supplemental layer on top.

So the Schools page does two jobs:
- **Maintain supplemental attributes** — the fields downstream systems need but HRAD doesn't provide.
- **Link the school to related domain data** — its **Attendance Area maps**, the **District Programs** it offers, and the **Municipal Divisions** it services. Those associations are made here, on the school.

A concrete example: an immersion **District Program** exists in its own area (S3.11) and a set of communities exists as **Municipal Divisions** (S3.10); on the Schools page you connect a given school to the programs it runs and the communities it serves. The program and the division aren't *created* here — they're *linked* here.

Every edit writes to DDH with the usual audit trail (S2.3) — `CreatedBy`/`UpdatedBy` and a soft-delete flag — so the change is attributable and reversible.

## Why it matters
If you think the Schools page *creates* schools, you'll go looking for an "Add school" button that shouldn't exist, or you'll try to fix an imported field here when the real fix is upstream in HRAD. Knowing the page is the **augment-and-link** surface tells you exactly what belongs here (supplemental attributes, associations) and what doesn't (the school's existence and its system-of-record fields).

## Watch out for
**"I'll create/delete the school here."** No — schools come from HRAD and SDM can't create or delete them. The page augments the existing record. If a school is missing or a core field is wrong, that's a system-of-record/sync question, not an SDM edit. What you *can* own here is the supplemental data and the links to Attendance Areas, District Programs, and Municipal Divisions.

## You've got this when you can…
- State that schools are imported from **HRAD** and that SDM **augments** them — it never creates or deletes a school.
- Name what the Schools page maintains: **supplemental attributes** plus **links** to Attendance Area maps, District Programs, and Municipal Divisions.
- Explain where a wrong *imported* field gets fixed (upstream/HRAD) versus a wrong *supplemental* field or link (here, in SDM, with audit).
